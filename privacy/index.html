Privacy policy modtechstudios.com

Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 November 2026.

ModTech is operated by Joseph Berhanu, a sole trader in Melbourne, Victoria, Australia. It is built local-first. This page explains what leaves your Mac, who receives it, and what we keep.

What the application sends

The ModTech desktop app goes online for the things below, and nothing else:

  • Licence check. When you enter your licence key, the app sends the key, a device identifier (a one-way scramble of your Mac’s hardware ID; it is the same every time on that Mac), and your Mac’s network name with its system type, for example “Mayas-MacBook-Pro.local · darwin arm64”. The network name often contains your own name. After that, while the app is open, it sends the key and the device identifier when the app opens, roughly every 30 minutes, and when you press Check again. If your subscription has ended and this Mac has no seat, it also sends the Mac’s name again each time it tries to rejoin. This confirms your subscription is active and that your licence is only used on the Macs it allows. We also look at these device identifiers by hand, across licences, when a refund is requested, to help spot repeated misuse of our refund guarantee.
  • Feedback, only if you send it. If you press the feedback button and send a message, we receive that message together with your licence key (if you have one), the app version, and your Mac’s network name and system type, so we can follow up. Nothing is sent unless you press Send (or Cmd+Return).
  • Links you click. On the licence screen, “Get ModTech” opens our pricing page in your browser and “Lost your key?” opens an email to us in your mail app. Nothing happens until you click.

Who receives it, and what we keep

  • Supabase, our database and hosting provider (our database is in Sydney, Australia), stores our licence records: your licence key; the subscription status, paid-through date and number of seats; Paddle’s reference numbers for your subscription and customer record (not your name or email); one record for each Mac you activate (its device identifier, its name, when it was activated and when it was last seen); any feedback you send; and a copy of each subscription notification Paddle sends us, which holds Paddle’s reference numbers, billing dates and price but no name, email or card details. Supabase and its infrastructure providers can see your IP address when your app contacts our licence service or you use the waitlist form or its confirmation page, and Supabase keeps its own request logs, including IP addresses, for about 7 days. Our waitlist entries are stored in the same database (see The waitlist). We also keep a log of your IP address and the time of each activation, seat-list, seat-release, feedback request, waitlist sign-up or waitlist confirmation, only to stop abuse. Entries older than a day are deleted the next time anyone activates a Mac, lists seats, releases a seat, sends feedback, signs up to the waitlist or confirms a sign-up, so in a quiet week an entry can stay longer than a day.
  • Discord. So we can respond quickly, our licence system posts alerts to a private channel that only the people who run ModTech can read: a new paid licence (Paddle’s reference numbers for the subscription and customer, the number of seats, and the last five characters of the key); a new Mac activated (its name); a change to a licence’s status or seat count, with the paid-through date; and any feedback you send (the first 1,500 characters, with the last five characters of your key, the app version and your Mac’s name). Confirming a waitlist sign-up posts only a running sign-up number (not a total of sign-ups), never anything you typed; an unconfirmed sign-up posts nothing. Your email address is never posted. Discord, run by Discord Inc., stores these posts on its servers, and they stay there until we delete them.
  • Email. Mail to contact@modtech.app passes through ImprovMX, an email-forwarding service that keeps a log of the sender and subject for 7 days, not the message. It arrives in a Google (Gmail) mailbox that we run for ModTech, as does anything you send us. Your licence key is emailed to you automatically through our email provider, Resend, a US company that sends from its Tokyo (Japan) region. Resend receives your email address, your first name if you gave it to Paddle, and the message, which contains your licence key, and keeps a copy for 30 days, stored in the United States. We do not keep a copy of that email in our mailbox. If a key ever has to be sent by hand, a copy stays in the mailbox it was sent from.
  • How long. A waitlist entry you never confirmed is deleted soon after its 72-hour link expires, by a daily clean-up. A confirmed entry is deleted by the same clean-up once 12 months have passed since you signed up (not since you confirmed), or sooner on request. The one-way hashes on our do-not-contact list, each with its date and a reason label, are kept indefinitely, so that an address that opted out is not added back by mistake. We do not delete licence, feedback or Paddle-notification records automatically. They stay until you ask us to delete them. A Mac’s activation record, including its device identifier, is removed when its seat is freed, by us at your request, or automatically when the seat is needed for a new Mac and it has not checked in for at least 14 days. Discord posts and mail in our mailbox stay until we delete them. Copies held by our providers expire on their own: Supabase keeps daily backups for 7 days and request logs for about 7 days, ImprovMX keeps its logs for 7 days, and Resend (once in use) keeps emails for 30 days.

What stays on your Mac

Your asset library, tech packs, measurements, images, and every other piece of garment data you create or import stay on your Mac. None of it is uploaded, synced, or sent to us as part of normal use. Your licence key and device identifier are also stored on your Mac, encrypted with the macOS keychain when it is available.

Payment data and your email address

We never see or store your full card number or security code. Payments are handled entirely by Paddle.com Market Limited, our Merchant of Record, who is the seller and holds your order, billing and tax details. Paddle may show us your card type, the last four digits and the name on the card. See Paddle's Privacy Policy for how they handle that data. When you subscribe, Paddle makes your email address and country available to us, and your name if you gave it, so we can send you your licence key and help you. We do not copy your email address into our licence records. It is held by Paddle and in our email systems. If you joined the waitlist, the address you gave us there is stored separately, as set out in The waitlist.

This website

This website sets no cookies of its own and runs no analytics of ours. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub and its content-delivery network can see your IP address when you load a page, and GitHub logs it for security. The pricing page also loads the checkout script of Paddle.com Market Limited. Paddle can see your IP address and browser details when you open that page, and may use cookies or similar storage for checkout, fraud prevention and its own analytics. Apart from that script, this site sends nothing of its own anywhere except the waitlist form when you submit it, and the confirmation page when you press its button.

The waitlist

Nothing joins the list through the form until you confirm it. When you submit the form we store the entry as unconfirmed and email you one link. Only opening that link and pressing the confirm button on the page it opens adds you from the form. A link works for up to 72 hours from when it was sent, unless it is replaced sooner. When we create a new link it replaces the earlier one, which then stops working, even if the new email does not reach you; if that happens you can enter your address again, but we send at most one email a day, so another may not follow straight away. An entry that is never confirmed is deleted soon after its link expires (see How long). We do this because anyone can type someone else’s address into a form, and we would rather send one email to the wrong person than add them to a list they never asked for.

For an unconfirmed entry we store the email address you give us, and the brand name and the “what you make” line if you choose to fill them in, together with an identifier for the consent wording shown with the form, the time you signed up, and a one-way hash of the link we emailed you. To run the list we also keep a copy of your address with any +tag removed and, for Gmail addresses, the dots too (so one inbox cannot be entered many times), which address of this website you used the form on (modtechstudios.com or www.modtechstudios.com), how many confirmation emails we have sent that address and when, a running number, a random id, and the time your link expires. We keep the identifier rather than a copy of the wording, so nothing typed into the form is stored as the record of what you agreed to. We record your IP address only to stop abuse, in the same abuse log described above and on the same schedule, not alongside your entry. Our database host also sees it in its own request logs, as described above.

When you confirm, we also store the time you confirmed and the IP address you confirmed from (an IPv6 address is cut down to its network part; an IPv4 address is kept whole). That pair is our evidence that someone with access to that mailbox pressed Confirm on a page that says how we will use the address, so unlike the abuse log it is kept for as long as the entry is.

These entries sit in the same Supabase database in Sydney, Australia as our licence records, in a separate table that is not readable by the website or the app. We use them for three things and nothing else: to send the confirmation email, to email you when ModTech opens, and to invite you to the paid pilot. We do not sell, rent or share them with anyone else. Supabase, named above, can technically access them as our host. We send waitlist email through Resend, the provider named above, which receives your email address and the message and keeps a copy for 30 days, stored in the United States. If you email contact@modtech.app directly to join by hand, that message is handled the same way as any other mail to that address — see Email, above. Confirming your address triggers a notification to the same private Discord channel described above that carries only a running sign-up number — never your email address or anything else you typed. An unconfirmed sign-up triggers nothing.

The confirmation email names us and says how to be removed: reply to it or write to contact@modtech.app. It has no unsubscribe link. If someone enters your address again we may send it again, no more than once a day, and a newer email replaces the earlier link, which then stops working. We send nothing else unless you confirm. We normally give a generic response rather than stating whether an address is already on the list, has opted out or was emailed recently. When our hourly confirmation-email limit is reached, submissions that would otherwise receive a confirmation email are asked to try again later instead. Responses are therefore not identical in every case. Every email after that one names us and carries a way to unsubscribe. You can also write to contact@modtech.app at any time and we will remove you, with no reason needed. We delete each confirmed entry 12 months after you signed up, or sooner if you ask.

When you ask to be removed, or unsubscribe, we delete your entry and keep a one-way keyed hash of your mailbox (your address without any +tag, and without the dots if it is a Gmail address) on a do-not-contact list. We cannot read an address back out of that list, and it holds only that hash, the date and a reason label such as ‘requested’; its only use is that if your address is ever entered again — by you or by anybody else — nothing is stored and no email is sent. We keep those hashes indefinitely, because deleting one is what would let you be added back by mistake.

No analytics in the app

ModTech does not include any third-party analytics or tracking SDK. The app never sends us your packs or library. If you book a setup session, email us files, or write about your work in feedback, we see what you show or send.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of, or the deletion of, the data we hold about you: your licence key and subscription record (including Paddle’s reference numbers for you), the names and device identifiers of your Macs, any feedback you sent, your waitlist entry if you joined, and the emails we have exchanged. Write to contact@modtech.app. We respond to privacy requests without undue delay and within the legal deadlines that apply, normally within 28 calendar days. If the law lets us extend that time, we tell you why and when to expect our response. Some records may have to be kept for legal obligations or legal claims; if so, we explain the limits on deletion. Deleting your licence ends your access, so we will cancel your subscription first. Copies in backups and at our providers expire on their own (see How long). Your order and payment records are held by Paddle, so requests about those go to Paddle too; we will pass yours on if you ask.

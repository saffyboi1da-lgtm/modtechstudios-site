03

What to know before you buy

  • Mac only. Mac with Apple Silicon. Tested on macOS 15.5; Intel Macs and other macOS versions have not yet been verified.
  • One seat is one Mac. Your Mac keeps its seat for as long as you hold the subscription, unless a different Mac activates while your Mac has not checked in for at least 14 days. Moving to a new Mac is self-service: if the old one has not checked in for at least 14 days, the new one takes the seat by itself, and you can always email us to free it sooner. There is no shared team library yet: each person has their own copy of their own library.
  • Your packs stay on your Mac. ModTech goes online only to check your licence and to send feedback if you choose to.
  • If your subscription ends you keep access to the end of the period you paid for, plus 7 days (what happens after a refund is set out in the refund policy). After that ModTech becomes read-only: you can still open your library and export your tech packs as PDF.

05

Billing

Monthly, quarterly and yearly billing are the same plan at three prices — US$29, US$78 or US$290 — charged upfront for the period you choose. Each renews automatically until you cancel. You can cancel at any time, with no fee, and you keep full access until the end of the period you already paid for, plus the 7-day grace in our Terms. Except where mandatory law or Paddle's Refund Policy requires or allows a refund, we don't refund the unused part of a quarter or year early — see what happens when you cancel, and your legal rights.

In Australia, New Zealand, the UK, Germany, France and the rest of the EU, any tax that applies is already included in the price shown. In the US and Canada, sales tax is added on top where it applies, so some buyers pay more than the listed price and some pay exactly that. The checkout shows your total, with the tax, before you pay.

Billing is handled by Paddle.com Market Limited, our Merchant of Record. Prices are in US dollars. If your card is in another currency, your bank converts the amount and may add a fee.

06

Setup session

Every purchasing customer gets one 30-minute ModTech setup session, included in the price, using one of your own packs. A purchasing customer is the individual or business that buys, not each seat.

  • One session in total. Renewing or resubscribing does not give you another.
  • It is a screen-share call. Bring one garment's flat and its measurements, and we will help you set ModTech up on that pack. We will not finish the pack for you or check it against a factory's specification.
  • Email contact@modtech.app to arrange it. Book and attend during your paid subscription period. Turning off automatic renewal does not end it early: you can use it until the paid period ends. The 7-day editing grace after that does not count.
  • If you ask while you are eligible and we cannot offer you a time before your paid period ends, or we cancel a booked session, your eligibility is extended until the session has happened.
  • You can reschedule once. If we cancel, that does not use your reschedule.
  • Having the session does not shorten your 14-day refund window on your first payment. If your subscription is cancelled because of a full refund or a chargeback before you have had the session, it is no longer available.

Refund Policy

Last updated 17 November 2026.

You can ask for a refund of your first payment within 14 days of making it. Refunds are decided and paid by Paddle.com Market Limited, our Merchant of Record, under Paddle's Refund Policy, which applies to every purchase whatever these pages say. Paddle may approve a request in full, approve it in part or decline it, and it declines requests where it finds fraud or refund abuse. This does not limit your statutory rights.

How to request one

You can ask Paddle directly, using the support link in your receipt or on your billing page, or at Paddle.net by choosing “Request refund”. You can also email contact@modtech.app from the address you bought with, and include your order number. Refunds are processed by Paddle.com Market Limited, our Merchant of Record, and returned to your original payment method where possible. Paddle's policy is to refund within 14 days of approving a request (and, for a statutory withdrawal, within 14 days of receiving your request). Please contact us or Paddle if you need help with a charge. This does not restrict your right to dispute a payment.

What happens to your licence

When we request a full refund of your current payment, we also cancel your subscription straight away. Editing then continues for 7 days after the cancellation is recorded (the grace period in our Terms), after which ModTech becomes read-only: you can still open your library and export your tech packs as PDF, but you cannot edit them or make new ones. Your work stays on your Mac exactly as you left it, and any PDFs you have already exported are yours to keep. We also review refunds that Paddle makes directly and arrange cancellation where appropriate. A refund on its own does not change your access; cancelling does. A refund relating to an earlier period does not automatically remove access you have paid for since. If a payment is charged back, we treat it like a full refund of that payment: we cancel your subscription and your access ends as described above.

Renewals and later requests

A request about a renewal payment is made the same way, and Paddle decides it under its Refund Policy. If a renewal was unexpected, contact us promptly. Nothing here limits your statutory rights.

Cancelling is not a refund

You can cancel at any time through Paddle's customer portal, or email us and we will cancel it for you. Cancelling stops future charges, and you keep access for the period you have paid for plus the 7-day grace in our Terms. It does not refund payments you have already made. Refunding a payment and cancelling a subscription are separate actions. When we refund a payment in full we also cancel the subscription, as described above.

Your legal rights

Nothing in this policy limits any right you have under mandatory consumer protection law, including the Australian Consumer Law. ModTech is intended for business use. Paddle's Refund Policy describes the withdrawal and refund protections that may apply to your purchase, including country-specific rights such as those in the United Kingdom and Canada; whether any of them apply depends on the law where you live and the circumstances of your purchase, and Paddle administers them directly. See Paddle's Refund Policy for the right that applies to you and the conditions attached to it. If ModTech has a major fault, you may be entitled to a refund or replacement even after 14 days, so please get in touch.

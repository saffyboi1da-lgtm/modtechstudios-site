Two ways to make the same pack

Less rebuilding between styles.

In a spreadsheet

  • Open last season's file and save a copy
  • Hunt the flat across drives, Finder and a chat thread
  • Retype the size chart
  • Re-letter the bill of materials for the new colourway
  • Paste the logo and rebuild the header
  • Check nothing from the old style got left behind
  • Export, and fight the page breaks

In ModTech

  • Start from a brand you saved
  • Right-click the empty slot — what you used before is at the top
  • Pull the measurements from a size template
  • Change the colourway once; the linked rows follow
  • Pull whole pages across from the last pack
  • Export the PDF

05

Why it exists

I spent a lot of time finding files in folders. Even when I knew the next techpack would share the same image, it was a circus just to locate it and get it into the pack. Do that across about a thousand packs, with assets scattered over drives and cloud storage, and it adds up.

In a spreadsheet, a techpack was most of an afternoon. In ModTech a new style is a fraction of that, and a style I have already built is faster still — that is the part the modules pay for.

It is three years of production work turned into a tool: modular, so you build the next pack from the last one instead of from nothing.

08

Questions

01 What is a techpack?
The document a factory works from: drawings, measurements, materials, trims, colourways and artwork for one garment. ModTech builds it and exports it as a PDF.
02 What does modular mean here?
Your brand, pages, size templates, colourways and asset library are kept outside any one pack, so the next pack is assembled from them rather than retyped. Change a colourway and the bill-of-materials rows and artwork that reference it follow. Drawings are images you place, so changing a colourway does not redraw them.
03 Mac only?
Yes. ModTech is a Mac app for Apple Silicon, tested on macOS 15.5. There is no Windows version.
04 Where are my files kept?
On your Mac. Your library and packs are stored locally and are not uploaded to us. ModTech does reach our licence service from time to time to confirm your subscription. Feedback is sent only if you choose to send it.
05 Can my team use it?
One seat covers one Mac, and each person keeps their own library. There is no shared team library yet. Leaving ModTech closed does not cost you your seat by itself: a Mac is released automatically only when a different Mac activates and the old one has not checked in for at least 14 days. Moving to a new Mac costs nothing and needs no new subscription, and if you need the seat sooner, email us and we will free it.
06 What happens if I cancel?
You keep full access until the end of the period you paid for, plus a 7-day grace period. After that ModTech becomes read-only: you can still open your library and export your packs as PDF. Your work stays on your Mac, exactly as you left it. A refund works differently: what it does to your subscription is set out in the refund policy.
07 When does it open?
We are not naming a date. Join the list and you will hear when it does.
